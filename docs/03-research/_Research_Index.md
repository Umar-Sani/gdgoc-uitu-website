## Why this folder is nearly empty

This project's decisions were made during a coursework build under deadline, mostly by picking
the option the team already knew or the one the frozen Build Guide named. Very little formal
comparison happened, and inventing research notes retroactively would misrepresent that.

The one topic here is open because there is a live defect (`BUG-001`) whose fix requires a real
choice that has not been made.

## Maintenance rule

Add a note here **before** the corresponding ADR, not after — the point is to capture what was
on the table while it still is. When the decision is made, link the ADR from the research note
and leave the note unedited as a record of the alternatives.
